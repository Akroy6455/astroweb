# What is Transit Weighted NDF Flow?

Astrology is a science of timing. While your birth chart (Kundli) shows the static potential of your life, the **Dasha system** combined with **Planetary Transits** shows when that potential will actually manifest.

At Tara Nirnay, we use a highly advanced algorithm called **Transit Weighted Net Dasha Flow (NDF)** to calculate your exact planetary periods.

## The Core Concept

Net Dasha Flow (NDF) evaluates the strength, awastha, and relationships of the Mahadasha (MD) and Antardasha (AD) lords. However, Dasha alone isn't enough. If you are running a brilliant Jupiter Dasha, but Jupiter is currently transiting through its debilitation sign in the sky, the results will be muted.

That's where the **Transit Multiplier** comes in.

We calculate the real-time transit of the Dasha lords through your birth chart, scoring their current dignity, awastha, and aspects. We then multiply the base NDF score by this transit score.

### Example:
- **Base NDF Score:** +85% (Excellent Dasha)
- **Transit Multiplier:** 0.6x (Lord is temporarily weak in transit)
- **Final Transit Weighted NDF:** +51%

By combining these two vectors, Tara Nirnay provides an incredibly accurate graph of your life's ups and downs, completely customized to the exact day.
